About the Institute

Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology

Established in 2007, NSIT is located at Amhara, Bihta, approximately 30 km from Patna, Bihar. The institute was established with the approval of AICTE and the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Bihar.

Its B.Tech programmes are affiliated with Bihar Engineering University, its diploma programmes with the State Board of Technical Education, and its management programmes with Aryabhatta Knowledge University. The ~9.2-acre campus provides laboratories, workshops, a digital library, auditorium, hostels and sports facilities.
